Our Story

Our Story & Chauffeurs

A vet-approved pet taxi and animal transport service built around how animals experience travel — through calm handling, climate comfort.

Who we are

Safe travel, made for four-legged family

PawVoyage began in 2019 when two veterinary technicians and a certified animal behaviourist asked a simple question: why is getting a pet to the vet, groomer, or airport so stressful? Our answer is a dedicated pet taxi fleet engineered around animal comfort — featuring whisper-quiet cabins, hydraulic ramps, and real-time GPS transparency.

Our journey

From one van to a citywide fleet

Key milestones along the road to safer animal transport.

  1. 2019

    First Vet Shuttle Van

    Two vet techs retrofit a single hybrid van to help local pet parents reach clinic appointments.

  2. 2021

    Custom Fleet Launch

    Purpose-built pet cruisers launch with HEPA air filtration, orthopedic suites, and hydraulic ramps.

  3. 2023

    Live GPS Owner Portal

    Real-time trip tracking, cabin temperature telemetry, and instant arrival photo check-ins go live.

  4. 2025

    18,000th Safe Arrival

    Over 2,400 devoted pet families served across metro and interstate routes — thank you!

Our values

What drives us

Four core principles guide every pickup, cabin layout, and hand-off in our fleet.

Patience over rush

No honking horns or hurried loading. Every ride starts with calm introductions so your pet boards willingly.

Vet-grade safety

Crash-tested harnesses, hospital-grade sanitization, and climate alarms keep every passenger protected.

Behaviour-led care

Designed with animal behaviourists to eliminate road vibration, harsh noise, and visual triggers.

Total transparency

Live GPS maps, cabin climate readouts, and photo check-ins connect owners to every mile of the trip.
