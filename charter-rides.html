Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about private pet charters and relocations.

Are charter rides private to my household?

Yes. Every City Charter, Regional Express, and VIP Interstate trip is 100% exclusive to your household pets — we never mix unfamiliar animals on charter bookings.

Can I ride along with my pet?

Absolutely. Up to two human pet parents are welcome to ride in our comfortable passenger captain chairs at no extra charge on any private charter.

How often do you stop on long-distance trips?

Our chauffeurs stop every 90 to 120 minutes at safe, quiet green spaces for leashed relief walks, fresh water, and litter box checks, sending you a photo update at each stop.

What if my pet needs medication during transit?

All PawVoyage chauffeurs are trained in veterinary oral and topical medication administration and follow your exact schedule logged in the owner portal.

What is your rescheduling policy?

Flight delays happen! Airport and regional charters can be adjusted free of charge when you share your flight number, and interstate trips can be rescheduled up to 48 hours prior.
