The Journey

How a PawVoyage ride works

Every booking follows the same calm, unhurried protocol — curbside greeting, climate-monitored travel, and verified hand-off.

1 · Curbside Greeting

Your chauffeur arrives on time, lets your pet sniff and settle, and fits a crash-tested harness or secures their carrier.

2 · Monitored Transit

A 68°F HEPA-filtered cabin with gentle acoustics while you watch the live GPS route and telemetry on your portal.

3 · Verified Hand-Off

Direct escort into the vet clinic, grooming salon, or home — with an instant arrival photo sent to your phone.

Passenger Size & Suite Guidance

Cats, Toys & Small Pets (< 20 lbs)

Medium & Large Dogs (20–85 lbs)

Spacious step-in suites with crash-tested Sleepypod harnesses, non-slip mats, and individual air vents.

Giant Breeds & Senior Mobility (85+ lbs)

Full-floor orthopedic memory-foam suites with low-incline hydraulic ramp boarding.
